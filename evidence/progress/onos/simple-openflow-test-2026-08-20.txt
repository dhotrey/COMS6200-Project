Note on last_error: the first connection attempt was made while ONOS was still
starting its OpenFlow listener. OVS retried and the session reached
state=ACTIVE, is_connected=true. The ptcp:6654 entry is Mininet's own local
passive listener, not the ONOS session.

===== FLOWS INSTALLED BY ONOS =====
ovs-ofctl -O OpenFlow13 dump-flows s1
  OFPST_FLOW reply (OF1.3) (xid=0x2):
   cookie=0x100007a585b6f, priority=40000, dl_type=0x8942 actions=CONTROLLER:65535,clear_actions
   cookie=0x10000ea6f4b8e, priority=40000, arp        actions=CONTROLLER:65535,clear_actions
   cookie=0x100009465555a, priority=40000, dl_type=0x88cc actions=CONTROLLER:65535,clear_actions

dl_type 0x88cc = LLDP, 0x8942 = BDDP. These are ONOS's link-discovery and ARP
punt rules, installed by the controller (not by us).
